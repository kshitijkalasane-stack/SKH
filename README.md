🌱 GramVikas

«🏡 A public transparency dashboard for village development projects.»

GramVikas is a full-stack civic-tech platform designed to make village development visible, understandable, and accountable.

Instead of citizens having to rely on scattered notices, word-of-mouth updates, or difficult-to-access records, GramVikas brings project information into one simple public dashboard.

«⚠️ Hackathon Prototype: All people, projects, budgets, locations, photographs, officers, and records shown in this application are fictional demo data and are not official or verified government records.»

🎯 The Problem

Village development projects involve public money, but information about them can often be difficult for ordinary citizens to understand or follow.

Citizens may not easily know:

🏗️ What projects are currently running?
📍 Where is the work happening?
💰 How much money was approved?
💸 How much has been spent?
📈 How much work is completed?
👷 Who is responsible?
👣 When was the site last inspected?
📸 What evidence exists?
⚠️ Which projects are delayed?
The result?

Less visibility → less awareness → less public participation.

💡 Our Solution

🌱 GramVikas

GramVikas creates a single public-facing record of village development work.

Citizens can:

🔎 Search projects 📊 Track progress 💰 View budgets 📍 Explore villages 👤 See responsible roles 🏁 Follow milestones 📸 View evidence ⚠️ Identify delayed projects 💬 Ask questions

The goal isn't simply to display data.

The goal is to make public information understandable.

⭐ Key Innovation

Project Transparency Timeline

Every project tells a story:

📋 Project Approved ↓ 🏗️ Work Started ↓ 👣 Site Visit ↓ 📈 Progress Update ↓ 📸 Evidence Added ↓ 🏁 Milestone Completed ↓ ✅ Project Completed

Citizens don't just see a percentage.

They can understand how that percentage was reached.
